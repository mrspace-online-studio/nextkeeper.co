Shipping and returns

Shipping

We ship within the United States only, from Venice Beach, California. Shipping is free on orders of $55 or more through December 31, 2027. Below that, US shipping is $6.99.

Returns

Every piece is one of a kind and is photographed before it is packed. All sales are final, with one exception: if a piece arrives different from its file (a tear we did not mention, a different color or size), write to us within 3 days of delivery with photos. We inspect the returned piece. If we confirm the difference, you choose an exchange or a refund. Worn pieces, and changes of heart, cannot be accepted. If a return is not approved, the piece is sent back to you at your cost.

Every story is true. Some more than others.
